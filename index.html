Hi, I'm Erin.

As a PhD Candidate in Political Science at Colorado State University, I specialize in American political behavior and political psychology. My research asks how trust and perceptions of risk relate to political attitudes, behavior, and the extent to which individuals are willing (or not) to challenge institutional norms.

My work is supported by the National Science Foundation (NSF), Rapoport Family Foundation, Microsoft Azure/OpenAI, and the International Foundation for Electoral Systems, among others.
